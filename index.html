Who We Are

An extension of the traffic police, drawn from the public.

The Tamil Nadu Police Traffic Warden Organisation trains civilian volunteers to assist Greater Chennai Police in regulating traffic, easing congestion at key junctions, and responding to on-ground road safety needs. Through the Road Safety Patrol, the organisation also works directly with schools — placing road safety education in the hands of students long before they get behind a wheel.

On the Road

Safety Rules Worth Repeating

Wear your seatbelt, every trip, every seat.

Hold your lane — discipline prevents pile-ups.

Slow down and switch on lights in bad weather.

Keep a safe following distance at all times.

Overtake only from the right, when clear.

Always give way to emergency vehicles.

Phones down — avoid every distraction.

Service your vehicle on schedule.

Respect posted speed limits, always.

Pedestrians: use the pavement, not the road.

Riders and pillions: helmets, no exceptions.

Stop fully at the line on red — every time.
